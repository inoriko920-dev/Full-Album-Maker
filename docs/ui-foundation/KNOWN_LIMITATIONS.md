# STEP 01 Known Limitations

- Workspace bodies are controlled placeholders by specification; visual/content parity of individual workspaces is not claimed until STEP 02–10. STEP 01 claims only the shared foundation/shell, navigation/state contracts, reusable controls, responsive behavior, status system, and screenshot harness.
- Exact v1.4.1 source remains unavailable. STEP 01 uses the recovered exact v1.4.0 source as the implementation baseline; the verified v1.4.1 portable remains behavior/build evidence only. No v1.4.1 source is reconstructed or guessed.
- The historical Windows v1.4.0 build workflow still references an upstream FFmpeg asset that returned HTTP 404 during STEP 00. STEP 01 does not silently rewrite that historical pin. Packaging/release remediation belongs to the later portable release gate.
- Windows Server 2025 CI now validates the STEP 01 focused suite and screenshot states at 100%, 125%, 150%, plus the 1366 px responsive breakpoint. Linux/offscreen CI separately validates all nine routes, exact shell landmarks, deterministic repeat capture, the recovered regression suite, and secret scanning. These tests reduce cross-platform risk but do not replace final real-Windows full-workspace golden comparison after the actual workspace contents are implemented.
- The screenshot evidence harness draws a deterministic Windows-like native title strip because Qt offscreen `window.grab()` captures only the client surface while the 1672×941 design references include native title chrome. Production keeps the real native Windows title bar; it does not render a duplicate custom title bar.
- Timeline host controls Split/Ripple/Snap/Marker are shared-shell placeholders and intentionally disabled until the Timeline workspace semantics are implemented in the later timeline step. Their presence in STEP 01 must not be interpreted as completed timeline editing behavior.
- The exact multi-megabyte golden PNG payloads were verified from the ASTRA source document, but are not checked into this branch through the text-oriented GitHub connector. Their exact SHA-256 manifest is committed. The screenshot harness refuses to resize a supplied golden and can emit 50% overlay/diff evidence when the exact files are supplied externally.
- Because the actual Beranda/Media/Album/Timeline/Visual/Template/Spectrum/AI Agent/Render bodies are intentionally deferred, whole-window pixel-difference against those finished-workspace golden references is expected to remain large during STEP 01. Structural shell geometry is the acceptance target here; finished workspace parity starts at STEP 02.
